## Scientifically grounded visual cues

The official whole-embryo figure shows a large anterior head, prominent yolk region, long tapered segmented trunk/tail, and primary fluorescent tumor near the yolk/perivitelline region. It does not resemble a symmetric adult fish with large dorsal fins. The film's bespoke larva should follow these proportions. Fluorescent tumor signal should remain a compact multicellular cluster rather than an amorphous glowing ball. Do not portray an immature zebrafish larva as possessing a fully developed human immune system.

## Message sources

- https://bioreperia.com/ — identifies ONCOLEADS with drug discovery and PREDICT with clinical decision support; states results within five days. Official slogan: “Changing the gameplan for cancer patients.”
- https://bioreperia.com/ztx-platform/ — transparent early zebrafish enable imaging fluorescently labeled human tumor cells.
- https://bioreperia.com/tumor-efficacy/ — human tumor xenografts in zebrafish, optional immune/stromal co-implantation, three-day treatment period, fluorescence imaging of tracked individual embryos, five-day assay output.

Use “Results in five days” as assay turnaround, not a promise of treatment benefit. Any procedural response animation should be identified as an illustration in the accompanying delivery notes and must contain no invented efficacy percentages or measured patient data.
